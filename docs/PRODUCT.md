# Product vision

## Problem

UBC Vancouver students don't always know which nearby study space has seats available. Existing directories and timetable tools don't necessarily tell them how busy a study zone is **right now**.

## Core experience

1. Open the mobile-friendly app.
2. See nearby study spaces, ranked by recommendation, reported busyness, or distance.
3. Compare recent reports and approximate walking times.
4. Choose a destination; optionally open external navigation.
5. Optionally submit a quick report while studying.

## Product principles

- **Simple first:** Minimize taps, forms, and directory-like navigation.
- **Busyness first:** The focus is whether a study space is worth visiting now.
- **Honest uncertainty:** Crowdsourced reports are subjective, not verified seat counts. Show unknown when evidence is insufficient.
- **Privacy by default:** Ask before accessing location; provide manual selection; avoid storing precise location unnecessarily.
- **Fast contributions:** Reporting should take seconds, with no forced account in the initial concept.
- **Accessibility:** Support keyboard navigation and clear labels; never communicate status by colour alone.

## Not yet decided

Naming, layouts, data sources, exact study zones, technologies, ranking and aggregation formulas, notification behaviour, hosting, and whether a map belongs on the first screen.

## Data warning

No fabricated live occupancy, independent report counts, or historical predictions. Verify opening hours and amenities before publishing.
